Teaching

I enjoy teaching and mentoring students, with a focus on building strong conceptual foundations and developing problem-solving skills in physics, astrophysics, and data analysis. My teaching philosophy emphasizes clarity, accessibility, structured learning, and cultivating independent research abilities.

Courses I Teach / Plan to Teach at CMI

  • Classical Mechanics
  • Electromagnetism
  • Quantum Mechanics
  • General Theory of Relativity
  • Astrophysics and Astronomy
  • Mathematical Methods in Physics

Specialized / New Courses

  • Methods of Gravitational-Wave Astronomy
    Introduction to GW detectors, waveforms, noise modeling, search pipelines, and Bayesian inference.
  • Numerical Methods in Physics and Astronomy
    Python-based numerical computation, ODE/PDE solvers, simulation methods, and HPC workflows.
  • Probability and Statistics for Physical Sciences
    Statistical inference, likelihoods, Bayesian reasoning, and applications to real astrophysical datasets.

Teaching Experience

I have taught and mentored students at ICTS, AEI, UCSB, and CMI. My experience includes tutoring advanced physics courses, supervising student projects, and co-teaching at research schools and workshops. I emphasize interactive learning, clear exposition, and research-oriented problem solving.

Student Mentoring

I work closely with undergraduate and graduate students on projects related to gravitational-wave data analysis, waveform modeling, population inference, and testing General Relativity. My goal is to train students to become independent researchers with strong computational and analytical skills.
