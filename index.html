Welcome

I am a researcher in gravitational-wave astronomy working on waveform modeling, compact-binary population inference, tests of General Relativity, and the development of advanced data analysis tools for the LIGO–Virgo– KAGRA (LVK) network and future detectors such as LIGO–India. My work spans search pipelines (IAS–HM), eccentric-binary detection, hierarchical inference, and robust GR tests.

This website provides an overview of my research, publications, teaching activities, and scientific talks.
