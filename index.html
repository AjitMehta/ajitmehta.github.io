Welcome

I am an Assistant Professor at the Chennai Mathematical Institute (CMI), specializing in gravitational-wave astronomy. My work spans waveform modeling of compact binary mergers, tests of General Relativity, gravitational lensing of gravitational waves, compact-binary population inference, and the development of advanced data-analysis tools for the LIGO–Virgo–KAGRA–LIGO India (LVK–LI) network and future detectors.

This website provides an overview of my research, publications, talks, and CV.
