Gravitational-wave astronomer · Chennai Mathematical Institute (CMI)

About

I am a faculty member at the Chennai Mathematical Institute (CMI), working on gravitational-wave astronomy, black-hole populations, and tests of General Relativity. I am a core developer of the IAS–HM search pipeline and work on population inference, eccentric binaries, and waveform systematics.

Research Interests

  • Gravitational-wave searches and higher-mode–aware pipelines
  • Binary black-hole population inference and hierarchical mergers
  • Tests of General Relativity with GW data
  • Future detectors: 3G observatories and LIGO–India
