Research

My research work has focused on building end-to-end, robust pipelines for gravitational-wave data analysis: from waveform modeling and searches in real detector noise to population inference and precision tests of General Relativity. With upcoming observing runs of the LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA (and in few years LIGO-India) detectors expected to deliver a rapidly growing volume of data, it is essential to develop analysis methods that are both computationally efficient and resilient to instrumental and modeling systematics.

My current research interests broadly encompass multiple areas of gravitational-wave science, including gravitational-wave astrophysics and cosmology, multimessenger observations, gravitational waveform modeling, gravitational lensing of gravitational waves, and tests of gravity using gravitational-wave observations.
